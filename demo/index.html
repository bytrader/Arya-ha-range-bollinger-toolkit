Interactive Heikin-Ashi & ATR-Normalized Slope Explorer

At a glance: this interactive tool converts price series into Heikin-Ashi candles, groups intervals, and plots ATR-normalized slope angles and threshold bands without look-ahead bias.

Visit botbama.com Watch Live — بات با ما (BotBama)

What You Are Looking At

Top pane: Smoothed Heikin-Ashi candles, moving average curve, and directional markers.

Bottom pane: Slope angle in degrees scaled by ATR(14) against configurable angle thresholds.

Dual-SeriesHeikin-Ashi smoothed candles paired with a lower slope & threshold pane.
ATR-ScaledSlope angles normalize price deltas against ATR so steepness stays scale-aware.
Zero Look-AheadEach bar evaluates only information available up to its own index.
InteractiveHover for bar metrics, scrub across history, and zoom in or out.
